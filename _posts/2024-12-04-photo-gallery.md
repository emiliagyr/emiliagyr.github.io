I am honored and excited to share that I received the Best Poster Award at the Neuropsychoanalysis Association (NPSA) Congress held in New York this past June 2025.

It was an incredible experience to present our work on the integration of micro- and macro-process approaches to psychic change. Engaging with such a brilliant community of researchers and clinicians and receiving this recognition from the NPSA is truly a highlight of my academic career.

A special thank you to my colleagues and mentors who supported this research. I am looking forward to continuing our work in bridging the gap between clinical practice and neuroscientific perspectives.
