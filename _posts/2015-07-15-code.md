A research team at the Pontificia Universidad Católica del Perú (PUCP) is leading a groundbreaking study to develop a neurocomputational profile for women exhibiting symptoms of Complex Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder (C-PTSD).

C-PTSD is a distinct diagnostic framework specifically associated with the experience of repetitive, prolonged traumatic events, such as domestic violence. Unlike traditional PTSD, this condition requires a more nuanced clinical understanding of how trauma reshapes long-term affective and cognitive functioning.

By utilizing a neurocomputational approach, our team aims to map the biological and cognitive signatures of this disorder. This research holds the potential to revolutionize our scientific understanding of the long-term impact of trauma on mental health and provides a critical foundation for designing more effective, evidence-based interventions tailored to the specific needs of women in Peru.
