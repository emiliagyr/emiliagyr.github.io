Online therapy in Spanish or English

Deciding to start therapy is the first step toward a more conscious and balanced life. I offer a safe, confidential, judgment-free space to understand your emotions, work through difficulties and build tools for daily life.

How can I help you?

  • Anxiety and stress: tools to regain calm and understand your body’s signals.
  • Migration and adaptation: support if you live abroad or face major life transitions.
  • Personal growth: a space to understand your behavior patterns and improve your relationships.

What it’s like to work with me

  • Personalized sessions of about 45 minutes, focused on your goals.
  • Evidence-based approach: I combine psychoanalysis with tools backed by neuroscience.
  • Flexibility: connect from home via Zoom or Google Meet.

To ask about rates and available times, write to me and we’ll arrange a session.

About me

I am a psychologist and researcher specialized in the study of emotions, social interaction and their neurobiological foundations. I bring my experience in clinical psychology, psychoanalysis and neuroscience into my daily practice.

  • Master’s in Psychoanalytic Clinical Intervention, PUCP
